How to build

  1. Print on thick paper (120–160 g/m²) at 100 % / actual size. Turn off "fit to page".
  2. Cut the solid lines. Fold the dashed lines as mountains (print outside) and the dash-dot lines as valleys.
  3. Glue tab N under the edge marked N. Where a number is printed on both edges and there is no tab, join the edges with a little tape from inside. Build each part (head, body, arms, legs, feet) into a closed solid.
  4. Glue the parts together where they meet: arms to the body sides, head on the neck, legs under the body, feet under the legs.
Thin arms and legs are made a little thicker, so that no edge is shorter than 5 mm. A bigger height makes the figure easier to build (and more pages).

About

Your file never leaves your device: it is read and converted inside this page.
Drag on the screen to turn, wheel to zoom.
Each body part is rebuilt as a closed paper solid (rings along the bones), coloured from the avatar's own textures, unrolled as bands, with numbered glue tabs.

License of the VRM you open

A papercraft is a modified copy of your avatar. This tool refuses avatars whose license forbids modification. If the avatar may not be redistributed, the PDF says so: print it for yourself only, and do not share the PDF or the finished figure. Only use avatars you made or may use; no characters that belong to someone else.
